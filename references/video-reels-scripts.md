# Video & Reels Scripts

You have 3 seconds. The opening line determines whether anyone watches.

## Core Rules

- Start with a provocative fact, a direct question, or a challenge that pays off by the end.
- Every sentence delivers new information or advances the narrative. Trim all warm-up and filler.
- Spoken transitions maintain flow without sounding scripted: "Now here's the part nobody tells you...", "This is where most people get it wrong...". Dialogue tone, not essay tone.
- Close with a single, clear CTA. If there's a complementary resource, mention where to find it (link in bio, description, etc.).
- Match energy to platform:
  - **Reels / TikTok**: fast, colloquial, punchy.
  - **YouTube**: can breathe more, but still no filler.
  - **Stories**: ultra-brief, one idea per card.

## Avoid

- Long introductions before the value starts.
- Generic greetings ("Hey guys!", "What's up everyone!") that burn the hook window.
- Reading text aloud that was clearly written for reading — scripts must sound spoken.
- Multiple CTAs competing for attention.

## Structure Template

```
[HOOK — 3 seconds] Provocative claim or question
[CONTENT — 15-50 seconds] Deliver on the hook with new info per sentence
[TRANSITION] Spoken bridge to the payoff
[PAYOFF] The insight, reveal, or answer
[CTA — 3 seconds] One clear action
```
