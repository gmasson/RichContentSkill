# Landing Pages & Conversion Pages

Clarity beats cleverness. Speed beats completeness.

## Core Rules

- Hero section communicates the core benefit in under 5 seconds of reading.
- Social proof near the CTA — most effective at the decision point, not at the top.
- CTA text describes action + outcome: "Download Free E-book", "Start Your 7-Day Trial" — never "Click Here."
- Minimize form fields. Name + email is baseline. Each additional field costs conversions.
- Trust signals near the form: "100% spam-free", "SSL secured", "Cancel anytime."
- One page, one goal. Multiple objectives fragment conversion.

## Avoid

- Hero text that requires reading the rest of the page to understand.
- "Click here" as CTA text.
- Asking for phone number, company, address unless absolutely necessary.
- Multiple competing CTAs on the same page.
- Pop-ups that fire before the visitor has read anything.
