# News & Current Affairs

Report facts. Provide context. Never editorialize.

## Core Rules

- Lead with the most important fact. Background comes second.
- Cite independent sources for controversial claims. Use "industry sources" or "official sources" when unnamed.
- If the story is developing: "This is a developing story. Information may be updated as new details emerge."
- Impartial tone: present divergent viewpoints with equal weight.
- Contextualize impact with specifics: "These changes could affect X million taxpayers by December."

## Avoid

- Editorializing or inserting personal opinion.
- Using loaded adjectives ("devastating move", "brilliant strategy").
- Burying the lead under context.
- Single-source stories presented as established fact.
