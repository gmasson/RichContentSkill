# Newsletters & Email Marketing

The subject line IS the product.

## Core Rules

- **Subject**: benefit or curiosity gap. Never "June Newsletter" or "Weekly Update."
- **Pre-header**: complements the subject line. Together they form a one-two punch that drives opens.
- First two sentences tell the reader exactly what they get from this email. No warm-ups.
- Offer value before asking. A free tip, a checklist, a useful link — reciprocity drives clicks.
- One primary CTA per email. Visually distinct. Competing CTAs fragment attention.
- Unsubscribe link must be visible and functional. Non-negotiable.

## Avoid

- Subject lines that are just labels ("Update", "News", "Info").
- Multiple CTAs of equal visual weight.
- Long paragraphs that look like walls of text on mobile.
- Sending without checking how the pre-header renders on popular clients.

## Subject Line Patterns

```
[Benefit]: "How to cut 40% of your time on reports"
[Curiosity]: "The mistake 90% of managers make during onboarding"
[Direct]: "Your launch checklist — ready to use"
[Question]: "Are you measuring the wrong metric?"
```
