# Social Media Posts

Three seconds to hook. Five seconds to hold. One action to take.

## Core Rules

- First line grabs attention without requiring context. If it only makes sense after reading the rest, rewrite it.
- Line breaks for rhythm. Short lines. Punchy fragments. Then a longer sentence that delivers.
- No emojis unless the user explicitly requests them.
- Questions drive comments: "What's your biggest challenge with...?", "Tag someone who needs to see this."
- Hashtags: 3–5, specific to the topic and community. Never generic (#success, #motivation, #grind).
- Platform tone calibration:
  - **LinkedIn**: professional, insight-driven, first-person okay.
  - **Instagram**: conversational, visual-first, captions complement the image.
  - **Twitter/X**: concise, opinionated, thread-worthy.
  - **TikTok**: colloquial, fast, trend-aware.

## Avoid

- Opening with "Did you know that...?" (overused, feels like clickbait).
- Emoji overload — maximum two per post, and only when requested.
- Hashtags in the middle of sentences.
- Generic engagement bait that doesn't relate to the content.
