# Case Studies

Problem, action, result. Everything else is trimming.

## Core Rules

- Open with the most critical problem and why it was urgent. Skip the company history.
- Only describe actions that directly generated results. Process details without impact are filler.
- Results: quantified, comparative, time-bound. "40% reduction in response time within 3 months."
- One impactful client quote with name and role.
- Close with transferable principles — what other readers can apply — not step-by-step instructions.

## Avoid

- Starting with the client's founding history.
- Listing every action taken regardless of relevance.
- Results without time frame or comparison baseline.
- Vague quotes like "Great partnership!" — prefer quotes with specifics.

## Structure

```
[Problem] What was broken, why it mattered
[Action] Key interventions (only what moved the needle)
[Result] Numbers, comparisons, timeframe
[Quote] Specific, named, role-identified
[Takeaway] Transferable principle for other contexts
```
