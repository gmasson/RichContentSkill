# Sales Copy & Copywriting

Sell the transformation, not the tool.

## Core Rules

- Lead with tangible benefits, not features. "Save 4 hours a week on reports" beats "automated reporting dashboard."
- Use mental triggers ethically:
  - **Urgency**: real deadlines only. Never fake countdowns.
  - **Scarcity**: actual limited supply. Never fabricated "only 3 left."
  - **Social proof**: verified testimonials with real names/roles.
  - **Guarantee**: transparent refund policy with clear terms.
- Imperative verbs create momentum: "Discover", "Claim", "Start", "Unlock". Vary them — three imperatives in a row feels aggressive.
- Temper promises with honesty. "Results may vary depending on effort and context." Credibility compounds over time.
- Open with a micro-story or analogy that mirrors the reader's pain point. Identification precedes persuasion.

## Banned Phrases

These patterns are banned in any language — detect the equivalent and avoid:

- "The ultimate solution" (without proof)
- "The best on the market" (without comparative data)
- "Revolutionary" (without explaining what it changes)
- "Don't miss this opportunity" (empty urgency)
- "Innovative product" (without describing the innovation)

## Pattern

```
Pain point (micro-story or scenario the reader recognizes)
→ Agitation (consequences of not solving)
→ Solution (your product/service as the bridge)
→ Proof (testimonial, number, case)
→ CTA (single, clear, action-oriented)
```
