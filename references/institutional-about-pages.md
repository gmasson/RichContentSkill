# Institutional & About Pages

Show the story, don't declare the values.

## Core Rules

- Genuine origin stories: "We started in 2018 when we realized that..." — never "We are a company that strives for excellence."
- Replace abstract values with concrete examples. Don't say "we value innovation" — describe the innovation.
- Quantify impact: "We reduced X tons of plastic waste in 2024" beats "we are committed to sustainability."
- Real team quotes or brief testimonials demonstrate culture better than lists of corporate values.
- Balance credibility signals (numbers, awards, certifications) with human elements (challenges, pivots, lessons learned).

## Banned Patterns

These are banned in any language — detect the equivalent and avoid:

- "We strive for excellence in everything we do."
- "Our mission is to satisfy our customers."
- "We are market leaders in..." (without market data).
- Any value statement that could apply to literally any company.
