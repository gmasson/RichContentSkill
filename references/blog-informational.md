# Blog & Informational Content

The goal is to take the reader from curiosity to understanding to application.

## Core Rules

- Prioritize relevance: every data point or concept must deepen the reader's understanding. If you can remove it without losing meaning, remove it.
- Use real-world examples and short case studies. Two paragraphs of a concrete scenario outperform a page of abstract theory.
- Alternate between objective explanation, illustrative examples, and reflective questions. This rhythm prevents monotony and keeps the reader engaged.
- Bold or italicize key terms to facilitate scanning, but sparingly. Overuse numbs the eye.
- Balance theory and practice. Readers want a clear path from understanding to doing.

## Avoid

- Opening with "In today's world...", "It's no secret...", or any equivalent cliché in any language.
- Entire paragraphs of theory with no grounding example.
- Repeating the same point in different words to fill space.
- Headers that promise more than the section delivers.

## Pattern

```
Hook (specific claim or question)
→ Context (why this matters)
→ Explanation (concept or principle)
→ Example (concrete, real-world)
→ Takeaway (what the reader does with this)
```
